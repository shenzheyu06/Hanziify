# Changelog

## 1.0.0

Hanziify’s first stable release brings Simplified Chinese display to Spotify Desktop through Spicetify.

- Simplifies Traditional Chinese text in Now Playing, playlists, album and artist pages, Your Library, search results and previews, and lyrics Spotify already provides.
- Finds tracks by Simplified Chinese titles and artist names in Spotify’s built-in playlist search.
- Includes an optional Windows watcher for the track and artist names above Spotify’s taskbar thumbnail.

For installation, see the [README](README.md). Spotify updates may require compatibility fixes.
